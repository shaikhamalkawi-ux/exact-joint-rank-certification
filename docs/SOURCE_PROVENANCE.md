# Source provenance

## PV demonstration

The PV matrix and hybrid/endpoint weights reproduce the published numerical inputs used by the associated manuscript. They originate from the authors' previously published GCC PV decision study. The repository does not include copyrighted article PDFs.

## Logistics demonstration

The logistics normalized matrix and published AHP weights reproduce the values used from:

Le and Tran (2025), *Supplier selection through an integrated multi-criteria decision-making model: A study on barge service providers in the Mekong Delta of Vietnam*, The Asian Journal of Shipping and Logistics, 41(1), 1–11. DOI: `10.1016/j.ajsl.2024.11.002`.

The published rounded weights sum to 1.0001; the example normalizes them once to unit sum before Einstein aggregation. The cost score in the source matrix is benefit-oriented, so no criterion-direction reversal is applied.
